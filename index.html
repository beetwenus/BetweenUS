A NOTE ABOUT BETWEEN US

Understand someone
without forgetting yourself.

BETWEEN US is a place to learn, reflect, communicate, and grow. We believe healthier relationships are built through understanding, honesty, respect, and effort from both people.

Mutual respectHonest communicationPersonal growth

BETWEEN US provides general educational relationship guidance. It does not replace professional counseling, mental health care, or support from trusted people. If you feel unsafe, threatened, controlled, or intimidated, reach out to a trusted adult or an appropriate local support service.
